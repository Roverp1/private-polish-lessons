# Nominative identification pattern was already fluent

In Lesson 0002 work, the learner correctly distinguished `kto?` from `co?`, used `to jest + mianownik` for people and things, and retained regular singular gender classification. The learner omitted several exercises because they repeated the same two sentence frames; the submitted representative answers were sufficient and showed that the target was below the learner's current level.

This establishes the fixed identification pattern, not broad case selection or unconstrained sentence production. Future lessons should stop distilled repetition after a few clean answers, combine inseparable systems when appropriate, and move sooner into contextual rule selection and error diagnosis.
