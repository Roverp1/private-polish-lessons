# Regular singular gender cues established

In the Lesson 0001 exit check and homework, the learner correctly classified regular singular nouns as masculine, feminine, or neuter from the common consonant, `-a`, `-o`, `-e`, `-ę`, and `-um` cues. The learner also explained that the dictionary form exposes the ending needed for classification.

This evidence does not establish how to recover a dictionary form from a plural or declined form, how to classify plural-only nouns, or how to produce independent sentences. Some Lesson 0001 tasks required those untaught skills and are excluded from assessment. Future homework must provide a short reading and a fixed sentence model before asking for production.
