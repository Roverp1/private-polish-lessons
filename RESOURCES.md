# Polish Resources

## Knowledge

- [Polish Grammar in a Nutshell — Oscar E. Swan (lektorek.org, PDF)](https://lektorek.org/lektorek/firstyear/nutshell.pdf)
  Canonical reference grammar by the leading Polish pedagogist at the University of Pittsburgh. Covers sounds, spelling, declension, conjugation, verb aspect, adverbs, syntax in compact form. Free. Use for: every grammar claim in lessons, the case system chart, verbal aspect reference. This is the primary source citations point to.
- [Lektorek.org — Oscar Swan's Polish learning site](https://lektorek.org/lektorek/)
  Swan's full site, hosts the Nutshell plus his "First Year Polish" textbook chapters and supporting materials. Use for: when a lesson needs deeper context than the Nutshell's compact form.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/learnpolish](https://reddit.com/r/learnpolish)
  Active subreddit for Polish learners. Use for: surface confusing items, get native-speaker intuition on edge cases, find what others got stuck on.
