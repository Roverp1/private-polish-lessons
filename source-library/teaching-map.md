### Recommended source use (20-25 min lesson; 10-15 min homework)

Use Maliszewski's **Przez osoby**, chapter I, *Już jest czas teraźniejszy!* Printed p. 9 / PDF p. **11** is headed **Czym się zajmujesz? Koniugacja -ę, -esz**. Exercise 1a supplies daily-life examples (`wstaję`, `biorę`, `myję`, `gotuję`, `piję`, `idę/biegnę`, `jadę`, `pracuję`, `piszę`); 1b is a connected routine with lexical choices; 1c asks for recall. Printed p. 10 / PDF p. **12** contains 2a word search, 2b `ja/ty` pairs, question/answer matching and occupation work. These two pages were extracted and checked against their visible printed labels. Their tasks combine vocabulary and case government, so select them by prerequisite rather than assigning every item. Locate conjugations by their endings, since Roman-numeral classifications vary among sources.

Suggested selection: explicitly teach how the supplied infinitive plus known `ja/ty` pair identifies the pattern; model two -ę/-esz verbs and contrast with target 13. Adapt 1b's contextual choice into 3-4 brief, familiar-context decisions, or take only a small representative subset from 1a/1b. Do not assign the page wholesale: exercises add lexical retrieval, case government, reflexives and varied patterns. Homework can use a few 2b pairs (dictionary-form recall only if taught), then a short message about a daily routine with no compulsory target quota. The full source exercise remains optional reference, not a prescribed page-length workload. In any adapted item, supply unfamiliar vocabulary and do not assess an ending family before modelling it.
